About us

Built by a Mumbaikar who wanted to make student life easier

Having grown up in Mumbai, I knew the city well, but watching my classmates navigate student life here made me realise how overwhelming it could be. From finding affordable places to eat and reliable study spots to figuring out everyday student essentials, there was a lot of information scattered around, and not always someone to point you in the right direction.

I hope this little initiative makes student life in Mumbai a little easier by bringing helpful information, local recommendations and useful resources together in one place. If it saves someone time, helps them discover a new favourite spot or makes their college experience just a little smoother, it has served its purpose.
